# Contributing

[日本語](CONTRIBUTING.md) | **English**

Review the terms of [BUSL-1.1](LICENSE). It is not an OSI-approved open source license.

This repository distributes verified releases. Bug reports, suggestions, and PRs are welcome, but accepted changes go through the usual verification in the maintainer's development environment and are included in a subsequent release. Development history and private original measurement records are not synchronized here.

The public snapshot includes runnable JavaScript and installation documentation, but not the TypeScript development tests or fixtures. Explain the purpose of your change and reproduce it with synthetic data. Do not test with existing production artwork or post materials you do not have permission to publish. Follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) and [Security](SECURITY.md) policy.
