# Support

[日本語](SUPPORT.ja.md) | **English**

Beta support is best effort. Check [installation](docs/install.en.md), [client-specific verification](docs/compatibility.en.md), and [recovery steps](docs/runbook.en.md).

Report ordinary bugs in a [public Issue](https://github.com/mhimt-lab/illustrator-studio-mcp/issues), including the version, checksum, environment, reproduction with synthetic data, expected behavior, and actual result. Do not include raw logs, execution records, local paths, artwork, licensed fonts, or personal information. Report vulnerabilities through the [Security](SECURITY.md) contact.
